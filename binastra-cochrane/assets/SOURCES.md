Reviewed on **15 November 2026, Asia/Kuala_Lumpur**. Downloads are bundled only for the requested local design preview. Public access to an image does not establish permission to republish it; confirm image-use rights before launch.

## Project and advisor references

- [Binastra Land project page](https://binastra.com.my/collection/binastra-cochrane/): developer attribution, contemporary residential character, Cochrane location and surrounding destinations. The registration form references “Binastra Cochrane Park 2”; this alone does not confirm the formal identity or approved particulars of the brief’s “Binastra Cochrane 2”.
- [Binastra Cochrane project website](https://binastracochrane.com.my/): neighbourhood context, proposed MyTOWN connection imagery, sustainability design intent and broad lifestyle themes. Specific floor plans and a facility schedule were not available in the reviewed page content. Its placeholder legal copy was not reused.
- [Bangsar Projects reference](https://www.bangsarprojects.co/): broad information structure and the user-approved advisor/business details: Premm Enbasegaran, REN62971, Affinity Realty Sdn. Bhd., registration details, business address and WhatsApp number. No Bangsar code, property specifications, prices, testimonials or project imagery were copied.
- [Monash University announcement, 28 October 2025](https://www.monash.edu/news/articles/monash-universitys-future-campus-in-malaysia4): a future Kuala Lumpur campus in partnership with TRX City. The website records the announcement date only and does not portray the campus as operating at TRX.

## Fonts

Cormorant Garamond (medium regular and regular italic) and Inter (regular) were obtained through Google Fonts, using local Latin-subset WOFF2 files. The accompanying SIL Open Font License texts are in `fonts/OFL-Cormorant-Garamond.txt` and `fonts/OFL-Inter.txt`.

The site does not contact Google Fonts when loaded. Font fallbacks remain available.
